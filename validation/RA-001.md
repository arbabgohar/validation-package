Rev A (draft), October 2026. Based on the Qaabil software requirements specification, Rev A. All scores are provisional until reviewed.

## Purpose

This document identifies what could go wrong with Qaabil version 1, rates each risk, and links it to the requirements that control it. The ratings decide how much teesting each requirement receives, as set out in the validation plan, and any gaps found become new requirements.

## Method

Each failure mode is scored before and after controls on three scales from 1 to 5. Risk score is severity times occurrence times detectability.

| Scale | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|----|----|----|----|
| Severity (S) | Cosmetic, no effect on records or decisions | Compliance gap that needs correction | Could contribute to a missed or late safety report, or to loss or exposure of regulated records |
| Occurrence (O) | Remote | Possible | Frequent |
| Detectability (D) | Always caught before harm | May be caught by routine review | Unlikely to be caught |

Severity 2 is an inconvenience with a workaround, and severity 4 is a significant compliance finding or data exposure. Occurrence and detectability 2 and 4 sit between their neighbors.

Risk levels: High is a score of 40 or more, Medium is 15 to 39, Low is below 15. Any failure mode with severity 5 also needs at least one control that does not depend on the AI agent and an independent review, whatever its score.

## Findings

Three gaps need new requirements:

- RUL-001: The system shall give each reportability rule set a version and effective date and shall record which version applied to every reportability decision and deadline.

- RUL-002: Changes to a rule set shall require an approval signature from an authorized role and shall be recorded in the audit trail.

- AGT-014: The system shall flag any complaint in a language the agent and keyword check do not support, and route it for manual review.

Two existing ratings should change. ENT-002 moves from High to Medium, since a feature leak harms the validated state but not a safety report. Every requirement tied to a High initial risk above keeps its high rating.

The highest residual risks are RSK-002 and RSK-024, both at 30. They rely on a person reading the output, so the test set and the reviewer training matter most for them.
