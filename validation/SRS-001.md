## Purpose

## This document defines what Qaabil version 1 shall do, so that each function can be designed, risk-assessed, and tested against a numbered requirement. In this document, "the system" means Qaabil.

## It covers only the functions released in v1, for English-language use: accounts and access, electronic signatures, audit trail, complaints, NCRs, CAPA, reportability rule sets, email intake, the triage agent, the reference library, and the controls around them. Later phases will be specified in their own documents.

## Version 1 supports English-language content only

## How to read this document

- "Shall" marks a mandatory requirement. Each has a unique ID that is never reused.

- Risk (R) is provisional: H, M, or L. The risk assessment will confirm or change it.

- Verification (V): T is test, I is inspection, D is demonstration, A is analysis.

- Requirements describe behavior and are independent of the technology used to build the system.

## Roles

- Company administrator: manages users, roles, and settings for one company

- Approver: signs reviews and approvals (for example, closing a complaint or CAPA)

- Contributor: creates and edits records

- Read-only auditor: views records and audit trails

- Vendor support: no access to customer records by default
