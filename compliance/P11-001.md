# Qaabil Part 11 control matrix

P11-001, Rev A (draft), October 2026. Based on SRS-001 Rev A and DS-001 Rev A. Items in \[brackets\] are open. The interpretation of Part 11 below must be reviewed by a qualified regulatory professional.

## Purpose

This document shows how Qaabil version 1 addresses each control in 21 CFR Part 11 on electronic records and electronic signatures. For every control it states who is responsible, how Qaabil meets it, and where the evidence is: the requirement IDs in SRS-001 and the design sections in DS-001. Customers can use it in their own assessment of whether Qaabil is suitable for records they must keep under Part 11.

## Scope and assumptions

- The matrix covers Qaabil v1, for English-language use, hosted in a US region.

- Qaabil is treated as a closed system, because each company controls who has accounts in its own workspace. Encryption in transit and at rest, and the signature controls, are applied in any case. Each customer decides how it classifies the system for its own purposes.

- Part 11 places some duties on the customer and not on the software vendor. Those are listed as customer responsibilities and are not claimed by Qaabil.

- Test evidence for each control will come from the test cases and test report (TC-001, TC-002, TR-001) and will be linked in the traceability matrix (TM-001).

## How to read the matrix

- **Who:** Qaabil, Customer, or Shared

- **Status:** Addressed in design, Shared, Customer responsibility, Procedure needed, or Not applicable in v1

|----|----|----|----|----|----|

## Gaps and actions

- Procedures: a vendor training and qualification procedure is still to be written for 11.10(i). The change control procedure for 11.10(k) is drafted as CC-001 and needs review.

- **Signature acknowledgement (proposed new requirement, ESG-006):** the first time a user signs, the system shows a statement that the electronic signature is intended to be the equivalent of a handwritten signature and requires acknowledgement. This supports 11.10(j) and 11.100(c).

- **System classification:** confirm with the regulatory advisor that treating Qaabil as a closed system, with the extra encryption controls, is a position customers can accept.

- **Equipment interfaces:** 11.10(h) must be revisited if instrument or equipment integrations are added in a later phase.

- **Evidence:** every row marked Addressed in design still needs passing test results before release.

## Customer responsibilities

Customers remain responsible for: validating Qaabil for their intended use, setting records retention periods, verifying employee identities, writing policies that hold individuals accountable for their signatures, certifying their signatures to FDA, configuring credential policies, managing who has accounts and roles, and testing any tokens or cards they choose to use.
