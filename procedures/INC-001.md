# Qaabil incident management procedure

INC-001, Rev A (draft), October 2026. Works with CC-001 for fixes and BR-001 for recovery. Items in \[brackets\] are open.

## Purpose

This procedure defines how Qaabil detects, handles, communicates, and learns from incidents, so that customers' quality records stay safe and customers know quickly when something affects them. Some customers' deadlines for regulatory reporting run while Qaabil is unavailable or unreliable, so clear and timely communication is part of the control.

## Scope

An incident is any event that harms, or could harm, the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of customer data or the correct behavior of Qaabil. It includes:

- Exposure of one company's data to another

- Loss or corruption of records, signatures, or the audit trail, including a broken audit chain

- Unauthorized access or suspected account compromise

- Outages and serious slowdowns

- A missed urgent flag or an unsafe or wrong output from the triage agent in production

- Failed backups or a failed restore

- A serious problem at a supplier that affects Qaabil

- **Communications owner:** \[name\]. Contacts customers. This may be the same person for a small team.

- **Independent reviewer:** \[name\]. Reviews serious incident reports.

## Severity levels

| Level | Meaning | Examples | Response target |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | Data exposure or loss, loss of audit integrity, or a safety-relevant failure | Cross-company data exposure, broken audit chain, a missed urgent flag in production, an unusable restore | Start within \[1 hour\], continuous work until contained |
| 2 | A major function is unavailable or degraded in a way that can affect deadlines | Outage, inbound email not processed, the agent unavailable | Start within \[4 hours\] |
| 3 | Limited impact with a workaround | A slow screen, a failed export for one record | Next business day |

## Procedure

1.  **Detect and record.** Sources include monitoring alerts, the audit chain check, sign-in alerts, backup failure alerts, and customer reports. Open an incident record with the time, the reporter, and a first description.

2.  **Triage.** Assign a severity and an incident lead.

3.  **Contain.** Stop the harm first. Examples: switch off the triage agent for one company or all, block an account, disable a feature through its entitlement, or take a system offline.

4.  **Preserve evidence.** Keep logs, audit entries, and affected data in a protected place before making changes.

5.  **Communicate.** Tell affected customers in writing: for severity 1 within \[period\] and for severity 2 within \[period\], with updates at a stated interval until resolved. Say what happened, what is affected, what the customer should do, and when the next update comes. Where complaints may have arrived during an outage, remind customers that their own awareness date and reporting deadlines are not paused, and that they should record complaints received outside Qaabil.

6.  **Investigate.** Find the cause and the extent, including which companies and records were affected.

7.  **Fix and recover.** Fixes follow CC-001, using the emergency path if needed. Recovery from backup follows BR-001. Re-test before restoring anything that was switched off, and for the agent re-run the agent test set.

8.  **Close.** Confirm with affected customers, update the record, and close it.

9.  **Review.** Within \[5 business days\] for severity 1 and 2, write a short report covering timeline, cause, impact, what worked, and what will change. Raise corrective actions in the change tracker. Update the risk assessment if the incident shows a risk that was missing or underrated.

## Agent safety incidents

If the triage agent misses an urgent event, proposes a clearly unsafe result, or is manipulated by hostile text in production, treat it as severity 1. The agent is switched off for the affected companies until the cause is understood, the fix is tested, and the full test set passes.

## Legal and regulatory notices

A data incident may create notification duties to customers, regulators, or individuals under contracts and laws. The incident lead asks legal counsel \[name or firm\] to assess this for every severity 1 incident, and the notification periods in customer agreements are recorded here once those agreements exist.

## Records and metrics

The incident record holds timeline, severity, cause, affected companies, communications, fixes, and review. Records are kept for \[period\]. Incidents are reviewed together every quarter to find trends.
